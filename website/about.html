About Nyirefami Limited

Our Heritage

Nyirefami Limited was established in 1981 as re-pack flour and other food stuffs. The company ownership is basically family enterprise with proprietorship registration of 1996 with certificate of registration from the Ministry of Industries and Trade No.50656.

Keys to Success

Our competitive advantage lies in our careful personal touch in raw grain preparation, washing, stone extraction, and processing clean flours for local and export distribution.
