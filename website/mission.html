Our Mission & Core Values

Our Core Mission

To process, mill, and package premium, stone-free cereal flours that provide wholesome nutrition to families across Tanzania and beyond, while maintaining uncompromising standards of hygiene and customer trust.

Our Vision

To be the leading household milling enterprise recognized for excellence in family health, infant nutrition, and sustainable agricultural processing.

Family & Baby Nutrition Commitment

Since our establishment in 1981, we have prioritized the health of nursing mothers and growing children. Our specialized flours like Finger Millet (Ulezi) and Wheat Semolina (Sooji) are carefully washed and milled to ensure safe, digestible, and nutrient-dense meals for every stage of life.
